What “training” means here
  1. The agent's first job is finding the right source. That's what is trained: the search ranking that feeds both modes.
  2. Each question is generated from a document, so the correct answer source is known (“What happened to the 10-year Treasury yield in 2022?” → the 2022 fact sheet; four distinctive words from a Buffett passage → that letter).
  3. The 80% training share picks the ranking settings with the best mean reciprocal rank. The 20% test share is never used for tuning; its score is the honest measure.
  4. Recall@3 matters most because every answer shows at least 3 options: it is the share of test questions whose true source appears among those options.
