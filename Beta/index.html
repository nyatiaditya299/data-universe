What this beta does—and doesn’t

This is a small, offline retrieval demo using public-source excerpts. It does not contain Fisher’s internal deal records, investment committee minutes, client data, or private decision history. It does not infer what Fisher’s investment team actually traded from an editorial article.

The replay rubric is a transparent self-review, not an AI grade. The AI step lives in Ask the archive: retrieval over a document library, with each answer checked against the passages it cites.

13F caution: the included holdings filings are quarterly snapshots reported after quarter-end. They omit rationale and are not complete portfolios; filings that postdate a scenario are only retrospective context—not information available to the trainee at the time.

This educational prototype is not investment advice, a security recommendation, or Fisher Investments’ official product. Public commentary represents the stated viewpoint of its author/editorial staff.
